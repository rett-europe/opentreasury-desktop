Review the frozen Saldo IPC contract (`app/src/shared/ipc-contracts/saldo.ts`) against the prototype UX spec. Determine if the backend needs amendment before Trinity's renderer can land the full redesign.

## Work Summary

Neo performed a field-by-field contract audit across three sections:
1. **KPI strip (Ingresos / Gastos / Saldo neto)** — all fields present, minor client derivations (net = income − expense).
2. **Monthly chart (Net series)** — current-year monthly net derivable, **prior-year monthly series missing** from contract. Hard gap.
3. **Category panels (Income/Expense breakdown)** — all fields present, soft policy note on per-category delta derivation.

**Key Finding:** ❌ Prior-year monthly series required for two-year chart visualization.

## Amendment Proposal (Decision B)

**Recommended:** Additive, non-breaking amendment to contract:
- Add `priorMonthlySeries: MonthlySeriesBucket[]` to `SaldoComparisonResponse`.
- Type reuses `monthlySeries` shape (existing `MonthlySeriesBucket` type).
- Backend computes prior-year monthly aggregates deterministically (same query path as `monthlySeries`, prior period).
- No impact on existing consumers.

**Rationale:** Enables two-year NET chart without client-side recomputation; aligns with "server-derived-fields" skill.
